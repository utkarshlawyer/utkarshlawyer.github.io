Utkarsh Kumar

Advocate · Founder, Aequis Legal

A litigation advocate whose practice is built on one conviction: that the quality of thought before the argument determines everything that follows.

Utkarsh Kumar is a graduate of Dr. Ram Manohar Lohiya National Law University, Lucknow (B.A. LL.B with Honours in Business Laws, 2021), one of India's foremost national law schools. He has been in practice since graduation and has built a substantive multi-forum litigation practice in under five years — an unusually rapid trajectory driven by consistent courtroom presence and rigorous legal preparation.

Utkarsh operates from his chambers in New Delhi, appearing regularly before the Supreme Court of India and the High Courts. He represents private individuals, corporations, and state governments, and manages a team of 30+ lawyers across Delhi and Uttar Pradesh across civil, constitutional and commercial matters.

His arbitration practice is among the most distinctive aspects of his work. He has been briefed on ICC international arbitrations and statutory arbitrations conducted before tribunals of the highest composition, including proceedings presided over by former Chief Justices of India. His infrastructure arbitration work, particularly in the road and energy sectors on behalf of national PSUs, has given him deep familiarity with complex, high-stakes multi-party proceedings.

Prior to founding Aequis Legal, Utkarsh gained formative litigation experience at the Allahabad High Court, sitting at Lucknow, where he built a broad and practical base across civil, service, constitutional and criminal matters in one of India's busiest High Courts.

He has represented leading public institutions including NHAI, GAIL, NMDC, NHIDCL, UPMRCL, AAI, RBI, HDFC, IIFCL and IIM Lucknow, as well as private corporations and individuals across the full range of civil and criminal litigation. His work in service law — representing government employees, pensioners and defence personnel before the High Courts — reflects a commitment to accessible representation regardless of the size of the matter.

Shireesh Kumar

Senior Advocate · Partner, Aequis Legal

More than three decades of advocacy across public institutions, regulatory bodies, development authorities, transport undertakings and banking disputes.

Shireesh Kumar has been enrolled as an advocate since February 1989 and has remained in active practice ever since. His work brings Aequis Legal a senior institutional perspective shaped by sustained engagement with public authorities, state corporations and regulatory forums.

He has been engaged as Special Counsel for U.P. Lucknow Nagar Nigam and the U.P. State Sugar Corporation, and has advised and represented public bodies in matters requiring practical command over administrative law, municipal governance, commercial disputes and statutory frameworks.

His institutional work includes engagements for Uttar Pradesh RERA, Uttar Pradesh Pradeshik Co-operative Federation, Lucknow Development Authority, U.P. Awas Evam Vikas Parishad, UPSRTC, U.P. State Road Transport Corporation, Allahabad Bank, IDBI and U.P. Metro Rail Corporation Limited.

At Aequis Legal, he strengthens the firm's public law, regulatory and institutional litigation practice, particularly in matters where experience before government bodies and statutory authorities is central to the strategy.
